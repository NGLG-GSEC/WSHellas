The Association, worldwide

The Widows Sons Masonic Riders Association (Widows Sons MRA) is an international fraternity of motorcycle-riding Freemasons, founded by Carl A. Davenport. Membership is open exclusively to Master Masons (Third Degree) in good standing with a regularly recognized Grand Lodge. Chapters ride together, support one another, and carry out charitable work — with particular care for the widows of Master Masons and their families, in keeping with the association's name.

Hellas Chapter

Widows Sons MRA Hellas Chapter is an independent Chapter of the Association, established and constituted with the valuable support and fraternal assistance of Widows Sons Bulgaria and of our American brethren — with particular and heartfelt thanks to Joe Norman «Storman» of Chicago, President of the American Chapter, and to Wilheim Neiszer «Wilhelm» of Mexico. The Chapter operates in full autonomy, while maintaining strong bonds of international friendship and mutual support within the worldwide Widows Sons network, and may propose the formation of new sub-chapters within Greek territory through its Chapter Committee.

Purpose

Chapter Hellas exists to protect and support its members and their families, and to assist the widows of Master Masons whenever the need arises. Members are encouraged to remain in good standing with their Lodge, to conduct themselves properly, and to take part in lawful activities that reflect well on Freemasonry. The Chapter undertakes at least one annual charitable donation, and a donation is made a matter of course at every regular meeting toward a cause of the Chapter's choosing. The organization takes no part in political, religious, or divisive activity, and no member may wear the insignia of a "1%" club while wearing the Widows Sons patch.

Membership

Widows Sons runs on Motorcycle Club custom. The Chapter is composed of Master Masons — Third Degree — recognized by the National Grand Lodge of Greece (ΕΜΣΤΕ) or another regularly recognized Grand Lodge who present a Certificate of Good Standing. A regular Grand Lodge must be recognized as regular by at least one of the Mother Grand Lodges — the United Grand Lodge of England, the Grand Lodge of Scotland or the Grand Lodge of Ireland. A brother who has not yet reached the Third Degree rides as a prospect: welcome in the Chapter, but not yet entitled to wear the full patch. Every candidate serves a minimum six-month petition period before being proposed for the Center Patch, followed by a further one-year probation before becoming eligible for full colors. Sponsors must present a candidate's case thirty days before any vote, and the award of the Center Patch requires a unanimous vote of those present.

Alongside full membership, an associated outreach initiative — Masonic Riders Hellas — welcomes recognized brethren who do not yet meet the requirements for full membership but share the association's values, so that they may take part in nearly all of the Chapter's activities without distinction.

The partners of members are known as Widows Sons Ladies. They are not Freemasons, and they do not join Widows Sons or wear its patches — but they're very welcome to ride and take part in the Chapter's social activities.

Rituals and Widows Sons meetings remain for members only.

  • Full Patch Member: a Master Mason (3°) in good standing who has completed petition and probation; wears the complete Widows Sons patch set.
  • Prospect: a 1° or 2° brother; rides and takes part in Chapter life, but does not wear the full patch.
  • Widows Sons Lady: the partner of a member; not a Freemason, and does not wear Widows Sons insignia.
  • Rituals & meetings: open to members only; Ladies join the rides and social side of Chapter life.

04 — Administration

SECTION 04 — Officers of the Chapter

The Chapter is administered by 15 officers. The first six positions constitute the Principal Officers; the remaining offices are optional.

The President, the Treasurer and the Road Captain are elected: the President for a term of five years, the Treasurer and the Road Captain every two years. The remaining appointments are made in accordance with the Chapter rules and the decisions of the President.

The official documents of the Chapter — its founding documents and the documents of the Secretariat — are issued from the Secretary.

05 — Emblem

SECTION 05 — The Chapter Hellas Emblem

Every element of the patch carries meaning — worn only by members in good standing, and always remaining the property of Widows Sons MRA.

  • Top Rocker
    "Widows Sons" — identifies the wearer as a member of the worldwide Widows Sons Masonic Riders Association.
  • Widows Sons
    The association's name in red script across the crown of the emblem, above the winged triangle.
  • Winged Triangle
    A triangle bearing the square and the All-Seeing Eye, flanked by outstretched wings — the eye watches over the Brotherhood, the wings carry its charity outward.
  • Twin Columns
    Two pillars set at the base of the triangle, echoing the pillars of the Craft Lodge.
  • Motto
    "Meet on the Level & Part upon the Square" — arcs beneath the triangle in red script.
  • Bottom Rocker
    "Hellas Chapter" — the Greek chapter's designation, worn beneath the central patch.
Rights to the Widows Sons name, emblem and insignia belong exclusively to Widows Sons MRA. Members hold their patches only for as long as they remain in good standing, and all insignia are returned to the Chapter upon a member's departure (Article 10).
06 — Constitution

SECTION 06 — Constitution of Chapter Hellas

Four articles establish who the Chapter is, why it exists, and who may belong to it.

Constitution of Chapter Hellas

Four articles · I–IV
  1. INameEstablishes the Chapter as Widows Sons Masonic Riders Association — Chapter Hellas, an independent Chapter in Greece, represented by a non-profit association.
  2. IIPurposeBinds the Chapter to the welfare of its members and their families, to the relief of widows, to at least one charitable donation each year, and to strict neutrality in politics and religion.
  3. IIIMembersAdmits Master Masons in good standing of a recognized Grand Lodge, fixes the petition and probation periods for the Center Patch and full colours, and provides for the Masonic Riders Hellas outreach and for honorary membership.
  4. IVWithdrawal & Reserve StatusAllows a brother to pass to reserve status for advanced age or health, and settles his voting rights and his reinstatement by vote of the members.
07 — By-Laws

SECTION 07 — Regulations & Operating Rules

Nine articles govern how the Chapter is run day to day: officers, elections, meetings, motorcycles, finances and discipline.

The Constitution and the By-Laws of the Chapter, kept as they should be — bound, shelved, and watched over.

By-Laws of Chapter Hellas

Nine articles · V–XIII
  1. VOfficersNames the fifteen offices of the Chapter and sets out the duties of each.
  2. VIElectionsThe President is elected for five years, the Treasurer and the Road Captain for two; the remaining offices are filled by appointment of the President.
  3. VIIMeetingsProvides for monthly regular meetings under Robert’s Rules of Order, with attendance required and decisions carried by a two-thirds majority.
  4. VIIIMotorcyclesRequires of every member a minimum engine displacement, a valid roadworthiness certificate, liability insurance and a Category A licence.
  5. IXFuneralsA standing committee of three attends the funeral of any member or close relative, wearing the emblem in tribute.
  6. XLogos & InsigniaHolds the patches as the property of Widows Sons MRA and permits them to be worn only upon the Chapter’s approved black leather vest.
  7. XIFinancesSets the entrance fee covering colours and regalia, the annual dues and charitable contribution, and exempts honorary members from mandatory fees.
  8. XIIMandatory EventsRequires one annual event or ride approved by unanimous vote, and fundraising events for the cause the Chapter has chosen.
  9. XIIIGrievances & DisputesRefers complaints and disputes between members, officers or chapters to the Round Table, upon written charges and a formal hearing.

Very Q∴H∴ Dimitrios, and dear Brothers of Widows Sons Chapter Hellas:

From the warm waters of the Caribbean and from the historic city of Cartagena de Indias, Colombia, please receive the fraternal and heartfelt greetings of Widows Sons Chapter Cartagena.

It is a true source of joy and pride for us to witness the creation of your Chapter in the land of Hellas.
